# Strand A — Philosophical / cybernetic research (parked)

## Scope

This strand asks how **eternal and classical knowledge traditions** — philosophical,
ontological, gnoseological, epistemological, and transcendental — can be related rigorously
to **cybernetics and contemporary technical practice**, including the conceptual armature of
*The Tao of Development* as a pedagogical corpus rather than as empirical evidence.

It is **not** educational research with student participants. It does **not** depend on
Front-End II coursework, consent forms, or the ethics-committee submission for Strand B.

## Status

**Parked (2026-09-25).** Work may continue as philosophical writing, corpus curation, and
studio reflection. No protocol for human subjects is opened here. No claim from Strand A may
be used to authorize or interpret Strand B data.
