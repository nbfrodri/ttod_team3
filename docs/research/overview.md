## Cover note

**Subject:** TTOD Research Initiative — Strand B pedagogical case (Front-End II)

Dear Colleagues,

Front-End II’s cohort builds the **TTOD Oracle Platform** as authentic, agentic-assisted
development work. Strand B asks whether that participation — as developers, in cohort —
functions as a learning activity with pedagogical outcomes (*learning by doing*). Strand A
(philosophical relations between classical knowledge traditions and cybernetics) is kept
**apart** and does not use student data.

## What this is / is not

**Is:** a design-based, rich-data case study layered on ordinary FE II assessment
artifacts; department pedagogical authorization asserted in advance; participant information
and consent templates in-repo; ethics review in progress; **congress-first publication plan**
(JENUI → ITiCSE/SIGCSE) with Profield STEM-code methodology inheritance.

**Is not:** a claim of effect sizes; Strand A empirical work; automatic conversion of all
coursework into research data; storage of signed consents or student PII in git.
